River Side FC

Welcome to River Side Football Club. Established 1982.

Club history

The club was founded in 1982 by local work dockers who started playing after their shift for fun but quickly became a serious team.

With only a football and muddy pitch the team quicly grew and attracted many supporters from their community and it became the heart of millbrook

As the club grew in its regional area it became well known for its resilliance and determination making their home stadium a fortress

Achievements

After many years in the regional leagues the club earned its first promotion in 1999 with major honors by being invincible in the league

In 2011 the club was promoted again to the highest division through promotion playoffs
